# Music

No music ships with this repo (licences don't allow re-sharing tracks). Put your own background tracks here
(`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`; mood sub-folders like `lofi/` or `upbeat/` are fine) and Claude will pick one and mix it
under your voice at export.

Free options:
- Ask Claude: *"find a free lofi loop for this video"*. It can search CC0 / CC-BY tracks with
  `.claude/skills/claude-video-editing/scripts/sound.py search "lofi" --music` (CC-BY tracks need a credit line in
  your video description; Claude will tell you).
- Sites with free licences for videos: Mixkit, Pixabay. Read their licence; most allow use in your videos but not
  re-sharing the files themselves.

Keep the files out of Git (this folder is ignored except for this note).
