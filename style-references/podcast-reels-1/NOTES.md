# Reference: "Turning podcast into reels" (Mark Suy, 16.6s) — clean modern talking-head
- Captions: 1–2 words at a time, white bold, lower-middle, no box; key words switch to red and grow;
  some words fill with colour progressively as spoken ("sounding", "same").
- Background switches every 1–2s, each tied to a phrase: flat illustrated landscape + speaker cut-out in
  a rounded card; blue gradient + small speaker cut-outs in coloured blocks; black grid + red highlight
  box + hand-drawn red underline; B&W cut-out with huge red text behind him; newspaper collage + red
  hand-drawn circle; beige paper with pure typography in mixed fonts per word (bold, script italic,
  heavy italic). Torn-paper/ink transition back to the face.
- Sound: steady music bed; swooshes on background changes; music DROPS OUT for a beat before key lines
  (1.3s, 11.5s). Captions/jump-cuts silent.
Implemented: minimal captions (`captions.style: "minimal"`, colour-fill key words), `behind` beats,
title `mark: "circle"|"underline"`, kinetic paper scenes, event-only SFX policy.
